04

Castlery AU journey scan

Castlery already competes at the top of the market on content and discovery. The gap is omnichannel, not storytelling.

The A+ content is excellent and sets product expectations almost at store level: UGC review imagery inside PDPs, the #AtHomewithCastlery gallery on the homepage, product videos, 360° & AR, shape diagrams and swatch close-ups.

Discoverability and financing are equally strong, with good page speeds, excellent filters such as Quick-Ship, and financing through no-cost EMI and BNPL.

Fulfillment is fairly good, anchored by Quick-Ship availability that delivers in 7–15 days. The real gap is the absence of omnichannel enablement: Castlery operates excellent physical stores that are not yet surfaced online, with no find-in-store, no store-stock visibility, no click & collect. Against the benchmark set, this is the single clearest place where leaders (Freedom, IKEA) pull ahead.

Contactability is also fairly good, spanning call, live chat, enquiry registration and book-interior-styling, with a clear opportunity to be a first mover on a "schedule a video call" feature.

Net read: Castlery is a content and discovery leader with a fulfillment ceiling. Closing the omnichannel gap and owning video-assisted selling are the two highest-leverage moves.

Omnichannel enablement

Castlery's omnichannel & fulfillment trails the benchmark leaders. Closing that gap unlocks uplift in both add-to-carts and conversions, which makes a Base or Optimistic single-digit % uplift credible.

Why +10.2% is credible

Derived from assumptions, professional experience and external evidence.

01

Shoppers are far more likely to buy when local stock is confirmed, and omnichannel buyers spend more once they visit the store through bundling and cross-buying.

Assumption + experience
02

Omnichannel conversion consistently beats default online. High-consideration purchases need multiple physical brand touchpoints, a pattern I have repeatedly observed.

Professional experience
03

Per Baymard checkout-UX research, an unclear delivery timeline and cost is a top cart-abandonment driver, and therefore a direct source of lost leads.

How we measure it

Primary metric
Incremental gross profit per 1,000 sessions — only margin-positive profit that would not have happened anyway.
Secondary metrics
ATC uplift, conversion uplift, and new-customer CAC decrement.
Guardrails
Count net-new orders only; exclude delivery-to-click&collect shifts where the feature simply moves an existing order; monitor staff bandwidth and collect-fulfillment SLA so omnichannel does not degrade showroom service.
Decision unit (grain)
Visitor-day for click & collect.
Target
Incremental conversion and revenue.
Attribution window
30–45 days.
Labels
Positive · omni order placedNegative · no omni order
Bias risks
Consult-bookers are already high-intent, which can overstate expected lift.

Field analogy: in restaurants, walk-in customers end up waiting when online-order flux spikes, or low-stock pieces sell out when a reserved live item is being shortlisted offline. The same showroom-service risk applies here, hence the staff-bandwidth guardrail.
